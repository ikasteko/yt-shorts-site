Privacy policy

yt-shorts is a personal desktop application used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

The application requests the youtube.upload permission only. It uses this permission solely to publish videos created by the owner to channels the owner controls.

The application does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never transmitted to any third party. No analytics or tracking is used.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
